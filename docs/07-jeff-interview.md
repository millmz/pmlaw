# 07 — Interview for Jeff

Answers to these questions directly shape how PAM is built — what it checks first, how it detects settlement packages, and what it flags as urgent. Short answers are fine; "in his own words" matters more than polish. Questions marked ★ matter most — if time is short, answer those.

Answer inline under each question, or just talk through it while Adam takes notes.

---

## 1. Your morning

1. ★ Walk through the first 15 minutes of your workday. What do you open, in what order, and what are you looking for?
2. ★ If you had an assistant who already knew everything in Smokeball, what are the first 10 questions you'd ask it on a normal day? Phrase them exactly the way you'd naturally say them.
3. What do you check at the end of the day, or on Sunday night, before the week starts?
4. What's the thing you most often discover too late? (A court date that snuck up, a task nobody did, an adjuster who never responded…)

## 2. Calendars

5. ★ What calendars exist at the firm? (Yours, Frank's, Anthony's, an office calendar, court calendar…) When someone says "the office calendar," what exactly do they mean?
6. Who puts events on calendars — you, staff, both? Does every court appearance reliably make it onto a calendar?
7. Are calendar events usually linked to the matter in Smokeball, or entered free-standing?
8. What kinds of events are on your calendar? (Court appearances, depositions, client meetings, closings…) Do you label them consistently — e.g. does a court appearance always say "court" or a judge's name?
9. Which courts do you appear in regularly? (Helps PAM recognize and group them.)
10. What counts as a "conflict" worth flagging — only two courts at once, or also court vs. deposition, court vs. client meeting?

## 3. Tasks and deadlines

11. ★ How are statute-of-limitations dates recorded in Smokeball? A task? A calendar entry? A word like "SOL" or "statute" in the title? Show one real example (name redacted is fine).
12. ★ Which deadlines are untouchable — the ones an assistant should never suggest moving? How would it recognize them by looking at the task? (Category, title wording, anything.)
13. Do you use task categories in Smokeball? Which ones actually get used?
14. Who assigns tasks to whom? Do your tasks get due dates reliably, or do some float without dates?
15. Honestly — how many overdue tasks are usually sitting there, and which kinds are genuinely overdue vs. "overdue but that's fine"? (PAM needs to know what to nag about and what to ignore.)
16. When you finish something, does the task get marked complete in Smokeball, or does it just get left?

## 4. Matters

17. ★ Roughly how many open matters does the firm have, and what's the mix? (X% personal injury, X% criminal, X% real estate…)
18. Do you use Smokeball's matter stages or statuses to track where a case is? Or is "where the case is" mostly in your head / in the documents?
19. How do you refer to matters out loud — client last name ("the Grasso matter"), case caption, matter number?
20. Are matters closed promptly in Smokeball when they're done, or do finished cases linger as "open"?
21. What does a "stalled" matter look like to you — how long with no activity before you'd want it flagged? 30 days? 60?

## 5. Settlement workflow (personal injury)

22. ★ Where do settlement packages live in the matter? Is there always a folder, and what is it actually named — "Settlement Package," "Demand," something else? Do older matters name it differently?
23. ★ Walk through your settlement process start to finish, on a real recent case: preparing the package → sending it → what happens next → how you track adjuster follow-up today.
24. How does the package get sent — email from Outlook (saved to the matter?), regular mail, both? If mail, is there any record in Smokeball that it went out?
25. Where is adjuster information kept — name, email, phone, claim number, policy limits? In Smokeball fields, in the documents, in your head?
26. After a package goes out, what's your follow-up rhythm? (Call after 2 weeks? 30 days?) What should PAM treat as "overdue for follow-up"?
27. What are the stages of a settlement negotiation you'd want tracked? (Sent → acknowledged → under review → offer received → countered → settled…)
28. What would you want in a one-screen "settlement status board" across all PI matters?

## 6. Email and documents

29. ★ Do you and staff reliably save emails to the matter in Smokeball (via the Outlook plugin), or do some conversations live only in Outlook inboxes? Roughly what share makes it into Smokeball?
30. Who emails adjusters — you, staff, both? From individual mailboxes?
31. Any consistent document-naming habits worth knowing? ("Demand - [client].pdf", dates in filenames…)
32. Are there folders that exist in every matter (Correspondence, Pleadings, Medicals…)? Is the folder structure standard or does it vary by who set the matter up?

## 7. People

33. ★ Who works at the firm and what does each person do? (Attorneys, paralegals, admin — names and roles.)
34. Who would use PAM after you? And is there anything — any matter type or client — that some staff should not see?
35. For each attorney: who is "their" staff — when you say "task my paralegal," who is that?

## 8. Judgment calls — teach PAM to think like you

36. ★ When everything is competing for your attention, what wins? Rank these for a normal morning: court appearances today, statute deadlines approaching, overdue tasks, settlement follow-ups, client calls to return, new intake.
37. What makes something urgent enough to interrupt your day vs. "put it in the brief and I'll get to it"?
38. What would make you *trust* this assistant? And what's the fastest way it could lose your trust?

## 9. When PAM can act (later phase — creating events and tasks)

39. Say out loud, naturally, how you'd tell an assistant to calendar a court appearance. (We'll use your exact phrasing as the test case.)
40. When you create a task for staff, what does it need at minimum? (Due date always? Category? Details?)
41. Default reminder preferences: how far ahead do you want reminders for court dates? For statute deadlines? (e.g. 6 months / 3 months / 1 month for statutes?)

## 10. Smokeball account (Adam may know some of these)

42. ★ What Smokeball plan is the firm on? (Bill/Boost/Grow/Prosper+ — it's on the invoice. API access may require Prosper+.)
43. Who is the firm's Smokeball account manager or main contact?
44. Do you use Smokeball desktop, the web app, or both? Windows or Mac?
45. Who administers Smokeball at the firm (adds users, changes settings)? They may need to approve the API connection.

## 11. The wish list

46. If PAM could do only ONE thing perfectly, what should it be?
47. What's the most annoying, repetitive thing you or your staff do in Smokeball every week?
48. Anything you've always wanted to know about your caseload but never had time to figure out?
